# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, reproducible, and protective of customer privacy. Discuss evidence
and behavior rather than attacking people. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, sustained disruption, impersonation, or publishing private information are not
acceptable.

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.
Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community. Use
GitHub’s reporting controls for conduct concerns. Vulnerabilities belong in private reporting
under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never in a public issue.
